    Support · Every gift carries a mission

    Power the network families rely on.

    DysNet runs entirely on volunteers, so a small gift goes remarkably far: it keeps the registers current, the registry moving, and a delegate in the room when European decisions are made.

    • Knowledge · hosting & translation of the four registers
    • Registry · the patient-owned data flagship
    • Voice · delegates where decisions are made

    The INAIL prosthetics workshop, Vigorso di Budrio. Photo: DysNet.

    Make a gift

    To DysNet Ideell Förening, non-profit, Sweden.

    Beneficiary: DysNet Ideell Förening, Solna, Sweden
    Reference: your name + “donation”
    Account details: confirmed by the treasurer before publication; request them at sal.giambruno@dysnet.org or info@dysnet.org.

    Online card payment arrives with the live site. Accounts are published after each AGM on the transparency page.

    Association membership

    €50 a year gives your association a vote and a voice, and your families the registers. An associate status without fees exists for associations with limited capacity.
